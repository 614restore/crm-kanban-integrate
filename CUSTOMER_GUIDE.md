## 1. Getting Started

TrussCTR is a web-based application accessible from any modern browser on your computer, tablet, or smartphone.

- **Logging In:** Use the email and password provided by your company admin.
- **First Login:** Complete your profile — add your name, phone number, and a profile photo.
- **Demo Mode:** Click "Try Demo" on the login screen to explore with sample data — no account required.
- **Forgot Password:** Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. A reset link will be sent to your email.

---

## 2. Your Command Center: The Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see when you log in — an at-a-glance view of your entire business.

- **KPI Cards:** Revenue, new deals, conversion rates, and open invoices in real-time.
- **Team Performance:** See how each team member is performing against their targets.
- **Activity Feed:** Live log of recent CRM activity — new contacts, notes, status changes.
- **Pipeline Value:** Total estimated value of all active deals.
- **Interactive Charts:** Click into revenue trends, lead sources, and conversion rates for deeper analysis.
- **Quick Actions:** Add a contact, create an estimate, or log a call directly from the dashboard.

## 3. Core Features

### Contact Management

- **Quick Add Wizard:** Add a new contact in 3 steps.
- **Full Contact View:** Details, communication history, documents, estimates, invoices, and project status — all in one screen.
- **Lead Source Tracking:** Track where every lead came from.
- **Insurance Information:** Store carrier, claim number, adjuster info, and deductible directly on the contact.
- **Search & Filter:** Find any contact by name, status, rep, lead source, or date.
- **Bulk Actions:** Update statuses, assign reps, or export multiple contacts at once.

### Pipeline / Kanban Board

- **Drag & Drop:** Move deals between stages (New Lead → Contacted → Estimate Sent → Won/Lost).
- **Custom Stages:** Tailor pipeline stages to your exact sales process in Settings.
- **Card Details:** Contact name, estimated value, assigned rep, and days in stage on every card.
- **Real-Time Updates:** The whole team sees changes instantly.

### Estimates

- **Line Items:** Detailed line items with quantities, unit prices, and auto-calculated totals.
- **Tax & Discounts:** Apply tax rates and discounts with automatic recalculation.
- **Send for Signature:** Email the customer a secure signing link.
- **Digital Signatures:** Customers sign in their browser — no printing required. Stored permanently.
- **Convert to Project:** One click converts an accepted estimate into a Project.
- **PDF Export:** Download or print a clean PDF of any estimate.

### Projects

- **Project Details:** Scope, start/end dates, budget, material cost goals, and subcontractor cost targets.
- **Status Workflow:** Planning → Active → On Hold → Completed → Cancelled.
- **Link to Estimate & Contact:** Every project is tied to its originating estimate and customer.
- **Budget Tracking:** Compare estimated vs. actual costs in real time.
- **Team Assignment:** Assign a project manager and crew members directly.

### Work Orders

- **Create Work Orders:** Assign specific scopes of work to team members (tear-off, installation, gutters, etc.).
- **Scheduling:** Set scheduled dates and track actual vs. estimated hours.
- **Status Tracking:** Scheduled → In Progress → Completed → Cancelled.
- **Labor & Cost:** Track labor costs per work order.
- **Start / Complete:** Field crew can tap "Start" and "Complete" to log real-time progress.

### Invoicing & Financials

- **Create Invoices:** Line items, tax, and auto-calculated totals.
- **Status Tracking:** Draft → Sent → Paid → Overdue.
- **QuickBooks Sync:** Sync invoices and customer data to QuickBooks Online.
- **Financial Dashboard:** Overview of revenue, outstanding balances, and payment history.

### Calendar & Appointments

- **Views:** Month, week, and day views.
- **Create Appointments:** Schedule site visits, inspections, and installs — linked to contacts.
- **Drag to Reschedule:** Move appointments by dragging on the calendar.
- **Team Scheduling:** See your entire team's calendar at once.
- **Color Coding:** Appointment types are color-coded for quick identification.

### Communication Hub

- **Log Calls:** Record call summaries, duration, and outcomes.
- **Email Tracking:** Keep records of important email correspondence.
- **SMS Log:** Track text message conversations.
- **Full History:** Every team member can see the complete communication history for any contact.

### Document Management

- **Upload Files:** Attach contracts, photos, permits, and insurance docs to any contact or project.
- **Photos:** Capture job-site photos from your phone, GPS-tagged, synced to cloud storage.
- **Templates:** Use document templates for common forms and contracts.
- **Quick Preview:** View documents without downloading them.

### Suppliers & Material Orders

- **Supplier Directory:** Store vendor contact info, pricing notes, and lead times.
- **Material Orders:** Create and track orders linked to specific projects.
- **Order Status:** Pending → Ordered → Delivered → Cancelled.

### Expense Tracker

- **Log Expenses:** Record project expenses with category, amount, vendor, and date.
- **Receipt Upload:** Attach receipt photos to expense records.
- **Project Linking:** Tie expenses to projects for accurate job costing.

### Automations

- **Trigger Events:** Fire when contacts change status, invoices are created, or estimates are accepted.
- **Actions:** Send in-app notifications, trigger email alerts, or assign team members automatically.
- **Toggle On/Off:** Enable or disable any automation without deleting it.

## 4. Role-Specific Guides

---

### Owner / Admin

You have full access to every feature. Here's what to focus on:

**Daily:**
- Review the **Dashboard** for KPIs, pipeline value, and team activity
- Check **Notifications** for automation alerts and team updates
- Review **Financial Dashboard** for open invoices and revenue

**Weekly:**
- Run **Pipeline Board** review — are deals stuck in any stage?
- Review **Team Performance** metrics on the Dashboard
- Check **Expense Tracker** for job cost overruns
- Sync **QuickBooks**

**Key Settings:**
- **Settings → Company Profile** — keep logo and company info current
- **Settings → Team** — add/remove users and assign roles
- **Settings → Integrations** — manage QuickBooks, Stripe, Twilio connections
- **Settings → Automations** — configure automatic notifications and actions
- **Settings → Billing** — manage your TrussCTR subscription

---

### Sales Representative / Estimator

Your job is to turn leads into signed contracts.

**Your Home Base:** Contacts + Pipeline Board

**Daily Workflow:**
1. Check **Pipeline Board** — which deals need follow-up today?
2. Log all contact activity in the **Communication Hub** (calls, texts, visits)
3. Update lead statuses to keep contacts moving through the pipeline
4. Create **Estimates** for every quoted job with detailed line items
5. Click **Send** — the customer gets an email with a signing link

**Closing a Job — Step by Step:**
1. Add contact via **Quick Add**
2. Set lead source and assign to yourself
3. Log your initial contact in **Communication Hub**
4. Create an **Estimate** with line items
5. Send for signature — customer signs electronically
6. Estimate auto-marks as **Accepted**
7. Click **Convert to Project** to hand off to the Project Manager

**Tips:**
- Use the **AI Assistant** to draft customer emails and score leads
- Add insurance info to contacts immediately — you'll need it for the estimate
- Track every interaction — your manager can see the full communication history

---

### Project Manager

You take over once an estimate is accepted. Your job is to deliver the job on time and on budget.

**Your Home Base:** Projects + Work Orders + Calendar

**Daily Workflow:**
1. Check **Projects** — review active project statuses
2. Review **Work Orders** — what's scheduled today? What's in progress?
3. Check **Calendar** — confirm crew appointments and site visits
4. Update project and work order statuses
5. Monitor budget — compare estimated vs. actual costs

**Running a Job — Step by Step:**
1. Receive project from Sales (converted from accepted estimate)
2. Open **Projects** → confirm scope, budget, and timeline
3. Create **Work Orders** for each phase (demo, installation, cleanup, etc.)
4. Assign work orders to crew with scheduled dates
5. Add **Calendar** appointments for inspections and crew arrivals
6. Update work order status as work progresses (Scheduled → In Progress → Completed)
7. Log **Material Orders** for supplies
8. Upload job-site **Photos** throughout the project
9. Mark project **Completed** and notify billing to send final invoice

**Budget Management:**
- Compare material cost goal vs. actual material orders on the project record
- Log all expenses in **Expense Tracker** tied to the project
- Flag overruns early — notify the owner before costs exceed the estimate

**Key Tips:**
- Create work orders the moment a project goes Active
- Upload before/during/after photos for documentation and dispute protection
- Use project **Notes** to log scope changes or homeowner requests

---

### Production Manager

You oversee day-to-day field operations across all active jobs. Focus on crew efficiency, scheduling, and quality control.

**Your Home Base:** Work Orders + Calendar + Projects

**Daily Workflow:**
1. Open **Calendar** — review today's scheduled work orders and crew assignments
2. Check **Work Orders** — confirm all today's work is In Progress or Completed
3. Review **Projects** — flag any jobs behind schedule
4. Check **Material Orders** — confirm materials are delivered for upcoming jobs
5. Verify crew is uploading field photos to each project

**Managing Your Crew:**
- Use **Work Orders** to assign tasks to specific team members
- Set scheduled dates and estimated hours per work order
- Track actual vs. estimated hours to identify efficiency gaps
- Log any homeowner field interactions in the **Communication Hub**

**Quality Control Checklist (per job):**
- [ ] Before photos uploaded
- [ ] Work order created for each phase
- [ ] Crew assigned and scheduled
- [ ] Material order confirmed
- [ ] During-work photos uploaded
- [ ] Work orders marked Completed by crew
- [ ] After/completion photos uploaded
- [ ] Project status updated to Completed
- [ ] Punch list items documented in project notes

**Material & Supplier Management:**
- Keep **Suppliers** directory current with contacts and pricing
- Create **Material Orders** as soon as project scope is confirmed
- Track delivery status on each order (Pending → Ordered → Delivered)

**Scheduling Tips:**
- Book crew assignments at least 3 days in advance when possible
- Use color-coded Calendar appointments to distinguish job types
- Add buffer time for weather delays — use the Weather integration to check forecasts before scheduling installs

---

### Office / Admin Staff

You keep the business running. Your focus is data accuracy, invoicing, and document management.

**Your Home Base:** Contacts + Invoices + Documents

**Daily Workflow:**
1. Ensure new leads are properly entered with complete info
2. Check **Financial Dashboard** for overdue invoices needing follow-up
3. Upload signed contracts, permits, and insurance docs to the correct contact/project
4. Log any calls or emails you handle on behalf of the team

**Invoice Workflow:**
1. Confirm project is Completed
2. Open **Financial Dashboard** → Create Invoice
3. Add line items from the project scope
4. Send invoice to customer
5. Follow up on unpaid invoices — log each follow-up call/email in **Communication Hub**
6. Mark invoice **Paid** when received
7. Sync to **QuickBooks**

**Document Filing Standards:**
- Upload signed estimates to the contact's Documents tab immediately
- Store insurance adjuster worksheets and claim numbers in the contact record
- Keep permit documentation attached to the project record
- Recommended file naming: `LastName_JobType_Date` (e.g., `Smith_RoofReplace_2026-03-01`)

**Tips:**
- Export contact lists from Contacts for reporting
- Log office and miscellaneous expenses in the **Expense Tracker**
- Keep the **Supplier** directory updated when vendor info changes

## 5. Settings & Configuration

Access Settings from the left sidebar (gear icon).

| Tab | What You Can Do |
|-----|----------------|
| **Company Profile** | Company name, logo, address, contact info, and email settings |
| **My Profile** | Your name, phone, avatar, and password |
| **Integrations** | Connect QuickBooks, Stripe, Twilio, EagleView, and Weather |
| **AI Assistant** | Configure AI providers and manage team access |
| **Notifications** | Control which events send you alerts |
| **Security** | Change password, manage two-factor authentication |
| **Billing** | View and manage your TrussCTR subscription |
| **API Access** | Manage API keys and webhook endpoints |

|-------------|--------------|
| **Draft Emails** | Describe what you want to say — AI writes it for you |

## 8. Mobile & Offline Use

- **Fully Responsive:** Works on any phone or tablet browser — no app download required.
- **Photo Capture:** Take job-site photos from your phone, GPS-tagged and cloud-synced when online.
- **Offline Mode:** The app works without internet. Data syncs automatically when reconnected. An orange banner appears when you're offline.
- **Install as App:** On most phones, tap "Share → Add to Home Screen" to install TrussCTR like a native app.
