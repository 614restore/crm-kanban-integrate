## Overall Tone Shift

### Before: 🎪 **Salesy & Overpromising**
- "#1 CRM" - unverifiable claim
- "500+ companies" - specific numbers without proof
- Feature-focused (what it has)
- Narrow focus (roofing only)

### After: 💼 **Professional & Confident**
- "Built for" - authoritative positioning
- No unverifiable claims
- Benefit-focused (what you get)
- Broader appeal (restoration industry)

---

## Brand Voice Guidelines

Based on these changes, here's the voice to maintain:

### ✅ **Do:**
- Be confident but not boastful
- Focus on benefits over features
- Use industry-specific language
- Be clear and direct
- Emphasize professionalism

### ❌ **Don't:**
- Make "#1" or "best" claims
- Use made-up statistics
- Over-promise results
- Use cheesy marketing speak
- Be overly technical
